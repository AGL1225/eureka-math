# Junior Explorer: Grade 4-8 Advanced Curriculum Roadmap

## Grade 4: The Mystery of Large Numbers & Shapes
1. **Place Value Palace:** Working with numbers up to 10,000.
2. **Mental Math Arena:** Quick addition and subtraction strategies.
3. **Decimal Dunes:** Introduction to tenths and hundredths.
4. **Fraction Forest:** Equivalent fractions and comparing sizes.
5. **Multiplication Meadow:** Multiplying 2-digit by 2-digit numbers.
6. **Division Deep:** Simple division with remainders.
7. **Pattern Peaks:** Growing and shrinking patterns.
8. **Shape Shifter Lab:** Identifying quadrilaterals and symmetry.
9. **Time Traveler:** Converting units of time (seconds to hours).
10. **Data Harbor:** Reading and creating stem-and-leaf plots.

## Grade 5: The Ratio & Rate Expedition
1. **Millionaire Mansion:** Working with numbers up to 1,000,000.
2. **Decimal Duel:** Multiplying and dividing decimals by 10, 100, 1000.
3. **The Fraction Bridge:** Improper fractions and mixed numbers.
4. **Ratio Reef:** Introduction to ratios and equivalent ratios.
5. **Percentage Park:** Understanding percentages as hundredths.
6. **Operator Oasis:** Order of operations (simple brackets).
7. **Geometric Grotto:** Measuring angles and identifying triangles.
8. **Transformation Tower:** Slides (translations) and flips (reflections).
9. **Capacity Canyon:** Estimating and measuring volume and capacity.
10. **Probability Pit:** Using fractions to describe probability.

## Grade 6: The Logic & Algebra Labyrinth
1. **Prime Peak:** Prime and composite numbers.
2. **Integer Iceberg:** Introduction to positive and negative integers.
3. **Ratio Runway:** Unit rates and real-world ratios.
4. **Algebraic Altar:** Using variables to represent unknowns (3x + 1 = 7).
5. **Coordinate Cloud:** Plotting points on a 4-quadrant grid.
6. **Surface Area Station:** Calculating surface area of prisms.
7. **Volume Vault:** Volume of triangular prisms.
8. **Circle Circuit:** Diameter, radius, and circumference.
9. **Mean Meadow:** Calculating mean, median, and mode.
10. **Pattern Palace:** Describing patterns using algebraic expressions.

## Grade 7: The Proportional Reasoning Realm
1. **Power Point:** Introduction to exponents and square roots.
2. **Integer Inferno:** Adding and subtracting integers.
3. **Decimal Den:** Operations with all types of decimals.
4. **Fraction Fortress:** Adding and subtracting fractions with different denominators.
5. **Percent Peak:** Calculating percent of a number (taxes, discounts).
6. **Equation Engine:** Solving multi-step equations.
7. **Angle Academy:** Parallel lines and transversals.
8. **Pi Planet:** Area of a circle and applications of Pi.
9. **Data Discovery:** Comparing different types of graphs (Box plots).
10. **Theoretical Theater:** Theoretical vs Experimental probability.

## Grade 8: The Linear & Pythagorean Path
1. **Scientific Sky:** Scientific notation for very large/small numbers.
2. **Integer Abyss:** Multiplying and dividing integers.
3. **Rational Road:** Operations with positive and negative fractions.
4. **Pythagorean Plaza:** Using the Pythagorean theorem in 3D.
5. **Volume Volcano:** Volume of cylinders, cones, and spheres.
6. **Algebraic Aviary:** Factoring and expanding simple expressions.
7. **Linear Lake:** Graphing linear relations (y = mx + b).
8. **Equation Everest:** Solving equations with variables on both sides.
9. **Geometric Galaxy:** Dilations and complex transformations.
10. **Data Science Station:** Correlation and lines of best fit.
